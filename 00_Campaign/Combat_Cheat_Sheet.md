# 1. The Core Roll
Roll: d20 + Attribute Modifier + Skill Bonus + Gear Bonus
Against: Target Number (TN) set by Narrator, or opponent's opposing roll
Floor Penalty: Current floor number is added to ALL TNs. TN 10 on Floor 1 = TN 11. On Floor 12 = TN 22.
## Success Tiers
Meet TN: Marginal success. You do it, barely.
Beat TN by 5+: Solid success. Clean execution.
Beat TN by 10+: Critical success. Something extra happens.
Miss TN by 5+: Hard failure. Consequences.
## Opposed Rolls
Both sides roll. Attacker must beat defender by 3+ to succeed. Margin of 0-2 is a stalemate. Beat by 10+ is a critical.
# 2. Attributes
Six attributes. All start at 4 (human average). 3 bonus points at creation. No attribute above 7 at start. Job provides +1 to two attributes.
STR: Physical power. Melee damage, carrying capacity, breaking things.
CON: Endurance and resilience. HP, potion cooldown, poison resistance.
DEX: Agility, reflexes, finesse. Initiative, ranged attacks, dodging.
INT: Knowledge, reasoning, magical aptitude. MP, spell power, crafting.
CHA: Presence, persuasion, performance. Viewer generation, sponsor negotiation, NPC reactions.
WIS: Intuition, perception, mental fortitude. Visible on sheet but not directly modifiable by player. Narrator uses for danger sense, intuition checks, mental resistance.
## Modifier Formula
Modifier = floor((Attribute - 5) / 2)
Attribute 4 = -1 | Attribute 5 = +0 | Attribute 7 = +1 | Attribute 10 = +2 | Attribute 15 = +5 | Attribute 20 = +7
## Base-100 Threshold
When any attribute hits base 100: legendary achievement + choose one of three permanent upgrades within 3 minutes. One option always makes future +1 count as +2.
# 3. Health, Mana, and Dying
## Hit Points
HP = 8 + (CON x 2) + Level
Example: CON 4, Level 1 = 8 + 8 + 1 = 17 HP. CON 7, Level 1 = 8 + 14 + 1 = 23 HP.
## Mana Points
MP = INT x 2
Regen: 1 MP per (20 - INT) minutes, minimum 1 per minute.
INT 8 = 16 MP, regens 1 per 12 min. INT 17 = 34 MP, regens 1 per 3 min. INT 20+ = 1 per min.
## Overcasting
When out of MP, spend Viewers instead. 1 Viewer = 1 MP. Incredible television; costs long-term economic power.
## Dying (Floors 1-3)
At 0 HP: Downed State. Unconscious. Stabilized by dungeon intervention before next encounter. Lose held items. Wake at 1 HP.
## Dying (Floor 4+)
At 0 HP: Dying. Each turn start, roll d20. 10+ = stabilize at 1 HP. 1-9 = death strike. Three strikes = permanent death. Allies can stabilize with INT check (TN 12 + Floor) or healing item.
## Sponsor Intervention
If Viewer count is high enough, a sponsor may save you. Costs entire Viewer pool + sponsor debt.
# 4. Combat
## Initiative
Roll: d20 + DEX modifier. Highest first. Ties broken by DEX score, then level.
## Actions Per Turn
Action: Attack, Cast Spell, Use Item, Dash, Disengage, Help, Hide, Interact.
Bonus Action: Off-hand attack, quick item use (potion), activate skill, or any ability tagged as Bonus Action.
Movement: Free and separate. Base 30 feet, modified by DEX and racial traits.
## Attack Resolution (Opposed)
Attacker: d20 + Attribute mod (STR melee / DEX ranged) + Weapon Skill + Weapon bonus
Defender: d20 + DEX mod + Armor bonus + Defensive Skill
Hit: Attacker beats defender by 3+
Stalemate: Margin 0-2. No damage, no advantage.
Critical: Attacker beats defender by 10+. Double damage dice.
## Damage
Magical weapons add enhancement bonus to both attack roll AND damage. Flaming Sword +2 = +2 to hit, +2 to damage, plus elemental effect.
## Potion Cooldown
Cooldown: 60 - (CON x 2) minutes, minimum 5 minutes.
Drinking before cooldown expires: 2d6 damage, nausea 3 rounds, effect halved.
CON 4 = 52 min. CON 20 = 20 min. CON 27+ = 5 min (floor).
# 5. Luck Dice
Pool: d6s. 1d6 per 5 levels (Level 1-4 = 1d6, Level 5-9 = 2d6, etc.)
Use: Add to ANY roll. Declared before or after d20 but before result announced.
Spend: Individually. Spend 1 for a nudge, burn pool for desperate swing.
Recharge: Full recharge after Safe Room rest.
# 6. Skills
Skill Bonus: Add skill level directly to relevant rolls. Level 8 Swordsmanship = +8 to sword attacks.
Starting Skills: 8-12 skills from pre-dungeon life. 1/3 useful, 1/3 situational, 1/3 hilariously specific.
Cap: Level 15 base (expandable by race/class features).
## Skill Advancement
Use-based. Mark each skill used meaningfully during the session. At session end, roll d20 for each marked skill. Roll ABOVE current level = +1.
Training Room: +3 to advancement roll
Guildhall: +2 to advancement roll
Legendary Skill Potion: Instant +1 level (bypasses roll)
Diminishing Returns: Gear/buff bonuses stack to ~14. Beyond that, extra points convert to advancement XP.
# 7. Leveling
XP to Next Level: Current Level x 100
Level-up grants:
- +2 Attribute Points (max +1 to any single attribute per level)
- +1 Skill Advance (choose one skill, auto +1, bypasses roll)
- HP recalculated: 8 + (CON x 2) + new Level
- MP recalculated: INT x 2
- Every 3rd level (3, 6, 9, 12...): choose 1 of 3 randomly generated Achievements
## Achievement Rarity
Scales with level: low levels mostly Common/Rare. By Level 20+ Epic and Legendary appear. Mythic possible at high levels.
## Floor 3 Milestone
End of Floor 3: Race and Class selection. Biggest power spike in the game. Before this, everyone is baseline human.
# 8. The Show
## Dual Fame Economy
Viewers (volatile): Current live audience. Spikes on spectacle, drops when boring or off-screen.
Followers (permanent): Dedicated fans. Crystallized from Viewers at Crescendo moments. Never decrease (unless truly despicable act).
Viewcount: Viewers + Followers. Determines loot tier, sponsor interest, AI attention.
## Crescendo Moments
Peak dramatic moments convert a percentage of Viewers to permanent Followers. Triggers: boss kills, sponsor contracts, dramatic last stands, incredible loot reveals.
## Sponsors
Between floors: choose from 3 sponsor contracts (rolled from pre-built pool via Chaos Engine). Each pairs a challenge with a reward. Optional. Completing a contract triggers Crescendo.
Under 50 Viewcount: Local sponsors, modest rewards.
50-200: Regional sponsors.
200-500: Galactic brands.
500+: Celestial-tier sponsors, game-breaking gear.
## Entertainment Value (EV)
Running assessment tracked by narrator. High EV = better loot, more sponsors, AI attention. Low EV = reduced loot, fewer sponsors, dungeon forces drama. The safest path is not the most rewarding.
# 9. Tag-Based Synergies
Every piece of gear has keyword tags (Fire, Ice, Shadow, Holy, Mechanical, Beast, etc.).
2 matching tags: +1 bonus
3 matching tags: +3 bonus + passive ability
4+ matching tags: +5 bonus + major ability
Tags create emergent builds. You become a fire specialist because the loot pushed you there, not because you chose it.
# 10. Equipment and Crafting
Slots: Main Hand, Off Hand, Head, Chest, Gloves, Belt, Boots, Accessory (8 total).
Crafting: Available Floor 3+ via Demolition Workshop in Personal Space. Break items into components, combine into new items. Quality depends on crafting skill + components.

# 12. Floor Structure Reference
Floors 1-3: Tutorial arc. No permanent death. Floor 1 = combat/loot basics. Floor 2 = skills/sponsors/training. Floor 3 = crucible, ends with Race/Class selection.
Floors 4-18: Permanent death. Each floor has: theme, boss, safe rooms, stairwells (Crescendo trigger), special events (via Chaos Engine).
Difficulty scaling: Floor Penalty (linear), Mob Scaling (~15% per floor), AI Attention (scales with Viewcount).
# 13. Personal Space
Pocket dimension accessible from Safe Rooms. Starts bare, upgraded with gold/materials. Upgrades provide daily buffs. Demolition Workshop for crafting (Floor 3+).

# 14. Floor 1 — Loyalty Card System (v1.1 addition)
Added by Floor 1 Build Session (May 18, 2026). Active only on Floor 1 ("Everything Must Go"). All Floor 1 NPCs issue a Loyalty Card on first interaction; cards stack into a per-Crawler Loyalty Tally tracked on the Crawler Dossier sheet.
Star earn: +1 successful CHA interaction or purchase; +2 helping NPC with side ask; +3 one-time per NPC for a "memorable customer experience" (narrator judgment).
Star loss: -1 rudeness or refused card; -2 theft; -5 killing the NPC or close ally.
Tally thresholds:
- 0-5 stars (Baseline): Standard prices, no bonus.
- 6-10 (Customer): 10% discount with that NPC, +1 floor-wide opposed CHA.
- 11-15 (Regular): 25% discount, +2 floor-wide CHA, NPCs warn of one chaos event before firing.
- 16-20 (Member): 50% discount, +3 CHA, one free Legendary-tier item from Pennybrook on first kiosk visit.
- 21+ (Charter Member): Boss-fight bonus — Walda takes -5 HP per Charter Member present, Garland appears as ally for 2 rounds.
Boss fight consultation: At fight start the narrator totals each Crawler's Tally and applies tier bonuses to that party member. This is intentional reward for engaging with Floor 1 as a place rather than as a loot box.

# 15. Chat System (v1.2 addition)
Added by Floor 1 Story Session (May 18, 2026). Campaign-wide mechanic, not floor-specific. Full spec lives in 00_Campaign/Chat_System.docx; this section is the cheat-sheet summary.
Crawlers, named NPCs (after first in-person meet), sponsors (after contract or cold outreach), and the dungeon AI (always) can exchange text messages via the HUD chat overlay. Format: 'Character (in chat): message.' Never radio conventions.
Range: unlimited within a floor; blocked between floors except via Personal Space (Floor 3+). Some later-floor zones may suppress chat; Floor 1 does not.
Privacy: chat is private by default. Audience sees only that messages are being exchanged, not their contents. Two Crawler-initiated monetization options: Open Channel (broadcast a thread live for +5d20 Viewers/minute) and Premium DM (accept a sponsor's bid to lurk in a private chat for a duration, in exchange for gold + reputation).
AI intrusion: the dungeon AI can DM any Crawler at any time, bypassing the contact list. Floor 1 default: AI is silent in chat unless a moment demands it (its voice arc is unestablished pre-play).
Narrator rhythm: chat pings should interrupt, not wait for polite scene breaks. Target 1-3 chat events per session, distributed unevenly. See Chat_System.docx §7.
Integration: chat-issued promises go in the Contract/Obligation Log. Chat interactions count toward Loyalty Tally the same as in-person ones. Looted chat history is a legitimate sponsor-monetization channel (logged in Reputation Ledger).
