# Floor 1 — Story Session Voice Notes (May 18, 2026)
Emotional register + 'never do this' per Voice Bible template. Build Session entries above provide dialogue samples and tone.
## Walda — register & shift discipline
Surface register: performing cheer over deep exhaustion; subtle erosion across the floor (wider pauses, occasional tonal flatness, quick recovery). Real register (boss room only): grief without self-pity — she is asking, not angry. Never do this: do not let the real voice leak before Phase 2; do not narrate Walda's interior at The Sears silence (the silence IS the foreshadow); do not let the surface voice carry real anger.
## Garland — register
Register: a man holding a job together because holding the job together is what he is for; competent until pressed. Never do this: no first-person references to his own physicality (no 'I'm exhausted,' no 'my back hurts'). His body is a fiction the dungeon maintains; if players notice the absence, that's the thread activating — do not lampshade it.
## Tasha — register
Register: armored deadpan over four-week-old grief she has not yet been told about; funny on purpose because the alternative is unsurvivable. Softens only when someone asks a question they took time to think of. Never do this: she does not narrate her own loneliness; pathos comes from what she says about the mall, the keychains, the socks — not about herself.
## Pennybrook — register (first-NPC pacing)
Register: warmth on autopilot; the warmth is real and is also a feature. 'Glitch' moments (forgetting his wife's name) land as small quickly-recovered stumbles — narrator does NOT call attention to them; players have to catch them. Never do this: no sales pressure or pushiness; as locked first NPC for Play Session 1, his job is to make the Loyalty Card mechanic feel like a gift, not a hook.
## Food Court People — registers
Sal: chosen useful over bitter, mostly succeeding; protective in a way that surprises him. Never do this: he does not swear AT a player — swearing is texture, not aggression. Dana: has watched a place she loves slowly become something else and decided to keep loving it; her stories are still happening in her. Never do this: she is not wistful in delivery; matter-of-fact — weight comes from the listener. Cinnabon Unit 7: discovering its sentience in real time, finds the discovery interesting not distressing. Never do this: no fluent grammar; sentence fragments are how it thinks, not malfunction.
