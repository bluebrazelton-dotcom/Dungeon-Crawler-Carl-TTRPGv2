STORY SESSION PROTOCOL
Connect the upcoming floor to the ongoing narrative. Review threads, update arcs, plant seeds.

# Load Phase
1. Master File Index — scan for current state.
2. Campaign Spine — review full narrative arc.
3. Thread Tracker — review all threads and pressure ratings.
4. Moments Log — review emotional history.
5. Voice Bible — review current character voices.
6. Character Index — know who exists and where.
7. Contract/Obligation Log — check for overdue obligations.
8. Reputation Ledger — review standing with factions/sponsors.
# Work Phase
1. Review Thread Tracker: update pressure ratings, note threads to advance on upcoming floor.
2. Review Moments Log: add entries from previous floor, retire paid-off entries. Enforce 30-entry cap.
3. Draft Campaign Spine entry for upcoming floor (three questions: story, advancement, emotional target).
4. Update Voice Bible: refresh dialogue samples, update emotional registers for characters whose arcs shifted.
5. Determine active cast for upcoming floor (4-6 characters). Others offscreen with noted status in Character Index.
6. Review Contract/Obligation Log: flag anything that should come due on the upcoming floor.
7. Generate Session Handoff for first Play Session on new floor (use template in 03_Sessions/, cap at 200-300 words).
8. Update any indexes or catalogs that changed.
9. Review and approve. Ready for Play.
